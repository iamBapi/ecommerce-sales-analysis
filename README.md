🎁 Gifting Sales Dashboard 2023 | Excel
An interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze gifting sales data and uncover insights into revenue performance, product popularity, customer ordering patterns, and seasonal trends.
The dashboard helps transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, PivotTables, slicers, and timelines.

2.	Business Questions Answered
This dashboard addresses the following business questions:
•	Which gifting occasions generate the highest revenue?
•	Which product categories and individual products perform best?
•	How do revenue and order quantities change over time?
•	Which day of the week receives the highest number of orders?
•	Which product categories perform best for each gifting occasion?
•	How do customer purchasing patterns vary across occasions and categories?

4.	 Key Insights
•	Seasonal Sales Trends: Revenue and order quantity peak in February and August, coinciding with Valentine's Day and Raksha Bandhan.
•	Order Distribution by Day: Sunday records the highest number of orders (176), while Wednesday has the lowest (112).
•	Holi Sales Performance: Colors account for 9.1% of sales during Holi.
•	Valentine's Day Performance: Soft Toys contribute 7.3% of sales during Valentine's Day.
•	Top-Performing Occasion: Raksha Bandhan (peak in August) 
•	Best-Selling Product: Magnam Set (~$120K revenue)

5. Dashboard Features
•	Interactive Slicers & Timelines: Filter sales data by quarter, month, date, occasion, and product category.
•	KPI Cards: Monitor total revenue, total quantity sold, and average delivery time.
•	Monthly Sales Trend: Analyze revenue and quantity trends over a 12-month period using a dual-axis line chart.
•	Occasion-Wise Revenue Analysis: Compare sales contributions across different gifting occasions using a donut chart.
•	Day-Wise Order Analysis: Identify the busiest and slowest days of the week using bar charts.
•	Top Products Analysis: Identify the products contributing most to sales.
•	Category vs. Occasion Heatmap: Explore category performance across gifting occasions using conditional formatting.

7. Workbook Structure
Worksheet	Description:
products	Contains product details and pricing information
orders	Contains order-level transaction data
Customers	Contains customer information
Supporting_Pivot	Contains PivotTables that support dashboard visualizations
Dashboard	Contains the final interactive sales dashboard

8.  How to Use
.	Download or clone this repository.
.	Open Sales_Dashboard.xlsx in Microsoft Excel 2016 or later.
.	Navigate to the Dashboard worksheet.
.	Use the available slicers and timelines to filter the data.
.	Explore sales trends, occasion-wise performance, product popularity, and order patterns.
Note: For the best interactive experience, open the workbook in the desktop version of Microsoft Excel.

9.  Business Value
This dashboard demonstrates how Excel can be used to turn raw transactional data into actionable insights. It helps identify seasonal demand, evaluate product performance, understand ordering patterns, and support data-driven sales and inventory planning.
